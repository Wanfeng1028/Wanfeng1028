I'm **Wanfeng (晚风)**. I write TypeScript for a living and in the evenings, and I'm slowly working my way into LLM agents, GIS tooling, and local-first software.

Most of what I've made is small — side projects I started to learn something and kept going longer than I meant to. Nothing here is finished, and I'd rather not oversell it. I'm mostly posting to keep notes where I can find them.

## Things I play with

- Small agent and AI tooling experiments
- Geospatial things — QGIS, GDAL, maps
- Audio: TTS, voice cloning, and the occasional voice assistant
- Blog and note-keeping setups

The [repositories tab](https://github.com/Wanfeng1028?tab=repositories) is the honest version of this list — most of it is toy projects, and most of the fork list is me reading other people's code.
